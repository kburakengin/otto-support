Otto

Car dashboard widgets for CarPlay, Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Otto fills your car's dashboard with widgets that belong there: a clock in the face you choose, your license plate, and a countdown to the dates that sneak up on you — inspection, insurance, service. It saves where you parked, and it can greet you through the car speakers when you get in.

Common questions

The welcome sound doesn't play when CarPlay connects.
iOS does not let an app create automations on your behalf, so this needs a one-time automation in the Shortcuts app. Otto's Setup tab walks you through it. The usual culprit is the automation being set to ask before running — open it in Shortcuts and make sure Run Immediately is selected.

My parking spot isn't saved automatically.
The disconnect automation needs the Save Parking Spot action, and location access must be set to Always — otherwise iOS won't let Otto read your location while it's in the background.

The widgets aren't on my car's screen.
On your iPhone: Settings → General → CarPlay → your car, then add Otto's widgets to the dashboard.
